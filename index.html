What events am I allowed to submit?

We welcome submissions for all kinds of community events, from informal get-togethers to large organized gatherings! 

If you're bringing people together, there's a good chance we'd love to hear about it!!!!


Some examples of events you can list include:

Social gatherings: BBQs, game nights, local meetups, and group outings.

Conventions and festivals: Furry conventions, community festivals, and other large-scale gatherings.

Special activities: Themed parties, workshops, discussion panels, art exhibitions, and performances.

Online events: VR meetups, virtual watch parties, and other internet-based activities.

These are just a few ideas! We're happy to feature events of all sizes and formats, provided they encourage a welcoming, positive, and inclusive community.

Is there anything that isn't allowed?

Most event topics are welcome, and we encourage a diverse range of activities and interests. There are, however, a few guidelines that all submissions and organizers must follow.

Adult-oriented events Disclaimer

Events intended for adults (18/21+) are allowed, but they must be clearly identified with an appropriate age restriction tag. Listings that fail to disclose adult content or age requirements may be removed without prior notice. Do not post a suggestive or sexual image for any 18+ events, all imagery should remain PG13. 

Prohibited content

We do not allow events or listings that promote or encourage:

  • Hate or discrimination against individuals or groups.

  • Violence, including threats or encouragement of harm.

  • Abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of minors or animals.

Our right to moderate

We reserve the right to decline, edit, or remove any event submission, listing, or organizer that we determine poses a safety concern or violates these guidelines.
